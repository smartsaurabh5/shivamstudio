ABOUT Shivam Studio and Photostate

Where Passion
Meets Professionalism

Shivam Studio and Photostate (Owner: Akhilesh Kumar Pal) is a premier luxury media production collective and document center in Jaunpur specializing in wedding films, commercial photography, drone videography, and photostate services. With years of visual excellence, we turn fleeting sentiments into archival pieces of art.

We combine state-of-the-art cinematic gear (4K/8K cameras, professional audio, stable aerial drone systems) and high-speed photostate services with dedicated craftsmanship to serve all your creative and documentation needs.
